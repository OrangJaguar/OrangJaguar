## Building

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### [Veridian](https://veridian.study/)

I never liked studying by rereading notes, forgetting everything, and calling it preparation.

So I built Veridian: a learning platform that turns your material into a structured path, identifies *why* an answer was wrong, and routes you toward the right kind of remediation—not just more random practice.

**Focus:** diagnostic learning, active recall, mastery tracking, spaced repetition

[Live product](https://veridian.study/) · [Source](https://github.com/OrangJaguar/Veridian)

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### [Plexity](https://plexity.tools/)

Everyday tools felt disconnected: tasks in one app, grades in another, PDFs somewhere else, markets in another tab.

Plexity is the system I wanted instead—a personal workspace that brings together tasks, grades, journaling, documents, planning, market tools, and an AI command layer.

**Focus:** productivity systems, full-stack tooling, data workflows, product design

[Live product](https://plexity.tools/) · [Source](https://github.com/OrangJaguar/Plexity)

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### [Scioly.app](https://scioly.app/)

As Science Olympiad president, I saw how hard it was for a team to coordinate preparation, resources, event work, and competition readiness in scattered docs and group chats.

Scioly.app is the platform I am building to give teams a better way to manage, study, prepare, and compete.

**Focus:** team workflows, study systems, competition preparation, education technology

[Live product](https://scioly.app/) · [Source](https://github.com/OrangJaguar/sciolyapp)

</td>
